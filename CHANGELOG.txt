Mineralogy 6.0.0.110021 for Minecraft 1.10.2 Forge

- Requires MMD OreSpawn 4.0.6 and makes OreSpawn the exclusive terrain, strata, ore, and fluid-deposit engine.
- Moves production Java packages to zone.moddev.mc.mineralogy while retaining all persistent registry and world identities.
- Adds the schema-4 Mineralogy provider with 32 rock rules, three mineral ores, Stable Layers defaults, Cyano migration defaults, and the covered Ocean crude-oil deposit.
- Adds the isolated mineralogy_crude_oil fluid, mineralogy:crude_oil block, and mineralogy:crude_oil_bucket with Forge fluid-container support.
- Migrates valid non-default legacy ore settings non-destructively and writes a deterministic upgrade report.
- Preserves existing mineralogy.cfg files byte-for-byte and writes content-only configuration for clean installations.
- Removes Mineralogy's internal geology, noise, stone-replacement, and ore-placement implementation.
- Backports the complete 17-locale Mineralogy 6 language set, normalizes translated geology and block-family terminology, removes inherited English fallbacks, and enforces exact 936-key parity and translation-quality checks.
- Preserves the lossless four-dust gypsum, chalk, and rock-salt conversions and adds Base Metals `dustCoal` support alongside charcoal, `dustCarbon`, and sugar gunpowder recipes.
- Leaves Minecraft's vanilla stone-slab recipe untouched, gives exact Mineralogy slab recipes precedence over generalized stone recipes, and adds lossless slab recombination plus brick and polished conversions for Mineralogy blocks, stairs, slabs, and walls.
- Restores the default-enabled `COBBLESTONE_EQUIVILENT` recipe compatibility for all 27 raw rocks and the historical gypsum, chalk, rock-salt block, and rock-salt lamp Ore Dictionary aliases.
- Restores the default-disabled `GROUP_TABS_BY_TYPE` option with five translated, searchable Mineralogy content tabs while retaining the legacy single-tab layout by default.
- Packages and safely exports the target-native player, content, developer, and provider guides plus the exact schema-4 Mineralogy provider on first use.
- Adopts the MMD four-component `Major.Minor.Bug.Target` policy, using deterministic Forge target `110021` in mod, Maven, CurseForge-display, manifest, and resource metadata.
- Documents OreSpawn's `terrain_dimensions` host lists as the resolution for issue #57, including new-world and existing-world configuration, 1.10 metadata matching, and new-chunks-only behavior.
- Documents OreSpawn's per-rock Preferred Y, Depth Spread, Minimum Y, and Maximum Y controls as the resolution for issue #55, including soft preferences, hard bounds, and saved-world scope.
- Documents OreSpawn's custom terrain dimensions and per-rock dimension membership as the resolution for issue #30, including Minecraft 1.10 numeric-ID mapping, dimension-specific rock sets and altitudes, and new-chunks-only behavior.
- Repairs saved rock-furnace loading so Minecraft can reconstruct the tile entity, restore its inventory and progress, and recover the rock-specific burn rate without changing historical NBT or tile IDs.
- Aligns novaculite with Mineralogy 6 hardness/resistance and makes double slabs drop two matching slabs normally or one matching full block with Silk Touch.
