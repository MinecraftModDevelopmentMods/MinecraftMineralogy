# Fresh-world geology balance

6.1.5 makes rock salt and gypsum less widespread while retaining substantial
dry-basin beds. Plains and sunflower plains have a stronger sedimentary-basin
preference; birch forests and mushroom fields can represent volcanic provinces.
These are game-scale proxies, not a literal geological model of forests.

Sulfur favours sedimentary and volcanic settings at Y -32..64; phosphorus uses
sedimentary/intrusive hosts at -16..64; nitrate favours shallow dry settings at
40..96. Their requested budgets are 12 blocks per attempt. OreSpawn remains the
only generation engine; vanilla management is not enabled by Mineralogy.

The six-seed combined-stack survey sampled 49,152 prediction columns and 96
random generated chunks per configuration, plus separate targeted samples.
In the sampled Y -60..63 natural rock, salt fell from 5.81% to 0.35% and gypsum
from 3.15% to 1.00%. These figures are not a guarantee for every seed or mine.

External overrides and global/world profiles remain authoritative. No retrogen
is enabled. Back up and deliberately edit an existing profile to change later
chunks; old/new chunk boundaries may then be visible.
